About the museum

Every bottle has a story inside

The Museum of Old Bottles began with a box of glass found during the restoration of an old caravanserai. Today it shows bottles and vessels from Rome, Persia, Central Asia, Europe and beyond.

Through these bottles we see how people stored water, wine, medicine, ink and perfume, and how glassmaking changed the world.

Good to know

Questions

Can I take photos inside?

Yes, photos without flash are allowed in every hall.

Can children try glassblowing?

For safety, children watch the show, but they can decorate their own bottle in the workshop.

Is there a museum shop?

Yes. You can buy handmade glass bottles made in our own workshop.

Can I donate an old bottle?

Yes. Send an email to info@museumofbottles.uz and our curators will reply.
